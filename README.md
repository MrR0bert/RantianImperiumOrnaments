# Rantian Imperium Ornaments mod for Anno 1800

Anno 1800 mod for [Rantius](https://www.youtube.com/@Rantius)

## Banner organization

With version 2.0 of the mod, all the banners have moved to a dedicated category in the beautification menu named "Banners".

Within this category the following banners are available:

``` text
Banners/
├── Rantian Imperium Banners/
│   ├── Logo Banner
│   ├── Naval Banner
|   ├── Airforce Banner
|   ├── Imperium Banner
│   ├── Eternal Sun Banner
│   └── etc.
├── Organizations/
│   ├── Belfort Banner
│   ├── New World Customs Unit Banner
│   ├── Phantom Cicrus Brigade Banner
│   ├── Flame Squadron Banner
│   ├── La Corona Banner
│   ├── Zandros Banner
│   └── etc.
├── Iris Garden Banners/
│   ├── Queendom of Sapin State Banner
│   ├── Isle of Remoray
│   ├── Isle of Poligny
│   └── Isle of Morteau
├── Republic of New Albion/
│   ├── New Albion banner 01
│   ├── New Albion banner 02
│   ├── New Albion banner 03
│   └── etc.
└── etc.
```
